What we measure

Answer type

Whether the line is answered by a touch-tone IVR, an AI voice agent, or a human.

Menu depth

How many layers of options a caller must pass through to reach their goal.

Hold time

How long a caller waits before reaching an answer or a person.

Why it matters for voice AI sales

  • Find companies still on legacy touch-tone menus: the clearest candidates for conversational voice AI.
  • Spot companies already using an AI agent, so you can qualify them out or position against what they have.
  • Open outreach with observed facts about a prospect's phone experience, such as menu depth and wait time.
  • Segment and prioritise accounts by how their phone channel really behaves.

How it's collected

Each record comes from placing actual calls to a company's published customer service number and recording what happens: how the call is answered, how many menu layers appear, and how long until a result. Observations are what we heard on the call, and phone systems change, so each record should be read as a snapshot of when it was measured.
